About Me

Curious by nature,
driven by growth

I'm Katerina, a Czech-born web developer based in Joensuu, Finland. I build websites and web apps using modern tools, and I'm currently doing a six-month web development programme at Riveria, where I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress and AI.

My path into tech wasn't the straightforward kind. A couple of years ago I started saying yes to new things, trying stuff I'd never done before, and slowly shifting from "I can't do this" to actually doing it. These days I'm part of a startup and collaborating internationally on a mobile app that's currently in development.

What I love most about this work is when something finally clicks and what was just an idea becomes something real. I'm open to freelance projects and always happy to talk to someone with an idea they want to bring to life.
